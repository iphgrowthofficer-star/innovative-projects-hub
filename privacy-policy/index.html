Privacy information

How this website handles information.

Last updated: November 2026. This page summarizes the current site setup and should be reviewed by the business owner before being treated as a final legal policy.

Information you provide

This website does not currently have a first-party contact database or an on-site email signup form. If you email IPH, your email provider and IPH may retain that message. Avoid including passwords, payment information, or sensitive customer data.

Third-party services

Booking links open Google Calendar appointment scheduling. The course waitlist is an embedded Google Form and asks for course interest, name, and email. Information entered into these services is handled under Google's terms and privacy practices. Review those policies before submitting information.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and is hosted through GitHub Pages. These providers may receive technical request information as part of delivering their services. IPH does not currently add a separate analytics or advertising tracking ID to the site.

AI tools used in training

When using AI platforms, only share information you choose to share. Review the platform's current privacy controls and terms. Do not enter sensitive, confidential, or customer-identifying information unless you understand and are authorized to do so.

Questions

For privacy questions, email iph.growthofficer@gmail.com.
