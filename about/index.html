Founder, Innovative Projects Hub

Hi, I'm Gina. I help make AI practical and understandable.

I've spent nearly 20 years in sales leadership, working alongside business owners and coaching team members across different roles. I've worked in retail stores and small businesses, and have seen how recurring tasks can pull owners away from customers and the work they do best.

I'm also a mom, so I know the juggle of work, school emails, practice schedules, dinner, and everything in between. Using AI tools such as Claude helped me find new ways to manage everyday tasks. Now I help small business owners, professionals, and individuals explore AI in ways that fit their work and life.

Why learn AI with Innovative Projects Hub?

Watching a generic tutorial can show features, but it may not answer how a tool fits your actual work. In a personalized session, we start from your questions and use examples relevant to your business or routine. I explain the steps in plain language and encourage you to check results before relying on them.

IPH offers training and consulting, not a promise that every task should be automated. We'll discuss what fits and what does not.
