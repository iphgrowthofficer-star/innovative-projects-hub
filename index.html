Frequently asked questions

Questions about getting started?

Clear answers, in everyday language.

Book your free 15-minute call

Do I need tech skills?

No. If you can send an email, you can use Claude. We'll take it one step at a time.

What is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant made by Anthropic that can help you write, plan, and organize.

Is my business information safe?

Only share information you choose to share. I'll show you where to review the privacy settings so you can decide what feels right for you. Avoid entering sensitive information unless you understand how it will be used.

Do I have to pay for Claude?

There's a free plan, and paid plans add more features. We'll look at your needs and choose what makes sense in your session.

Are sessions online or in person?

Sessions are available online or in person.

What's the difference between a Starter Session and an Agent Build?

A Starter Session helps you learn Claude and use it for everyday tasks. An Agent Build focuses on one repetitive process and guides you through making a custom Claude agent for it. It's best to start with a Starter Session.
