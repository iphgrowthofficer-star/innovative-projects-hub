THE SMALL BUSINESS AI OPPORTUNITY CHECKLIST

A practical worksheet to spot everyday tasks you may be able to simplify with AI. Start with low-risk work, review every result, and do not enter sensitive customer or business information unless you understand the tool's privacy settings.

MARKETING
[ ] Brainstorm a month's worth of post topics from services you already offer.
[ ] Draft a first version of a newsletter using notes you provide.
[ ] Adapt one approved announcement for email, a social post, and a flyer.

CUSTOMER SERVICE
[ ] Draft a friendly response to a common customer question.
[ ] Turn service details into a clear FAQ for customers.
[ ] Rewrite a message to sound warmer, shorter, or easier to understand.

ADMINISTRATION
[ ] Turn meeting notes into a task list with owners and due dates.
[ ] Draft a reusable checklist for a task you repeat each week.
[ ] Summarize a non-sensitive document into key points and questions.

SALES
[ ] Draft a follow-up message after an inquiry using facts you verify.
[ ] Organize common customer questions into themes.

SCHEDULING
[ ] Draft a weekly planning template around your regular commitments.
[ ] Turn a list of tasks into a suggested schedule for you to review.

ORGANIZATION
[ ] Create a folder or file naming guide for your team.
[ ] Turn a process you describe into a step-by-step first draft.

A SIMPLE FIRST STEP
Choose one repetitive, low-risk task. Give the AI tool clear context, ask for a draft, check it for accuracy, and edit it before using it. AI can make mistakes; you remain responsible for reviewing its work.
