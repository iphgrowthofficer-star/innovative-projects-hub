Questions & answers

Common questions about learning and using AI.

Straightforward information to help you decide whether training or consulting is right for you.

Do I need technical experience?

No. Sessions start with your goals and comfort level and explain the steps in plain language.

Do you only teach Claude?

No. Claude is an important current training option. ChatGPT and other platforms can be discussed depending on your goals and the tools' available features.

What happens during the free 15-minute call?

We talk about your business or routine, the work you want help with, and whether training or consulting seems like a useful next step. There is no obligation to book a paid service.

Are sessions online or in person?

Sessions are available online and in person. In-person arrangements are made directly; no public office address is listed.

Can AI make mistakes?

Yes. AI tools may produce incorrect, incomplete, or outdated responses. Review outputs, verify important facts, and use your judgment before acting.

Is my business information safe?

Only share information you choose to share. Review the AI tool's privacy settings and policies. Avoid sharing sensitive or confidential information unless you understand how it will be handled.

Do I have to pay for Claude or ChatGPT?

AI platforms may offer free and paid plans, and plan details can change. Check each provider's current plans. We'll discuss what fits your needs in training.

What's the difference between training and a custom agent?

Training helps you learn to use AI tools. Agent consultation maps one repetitive process and plans a focused assistant. Building, connecting to external accounts, testing, and deployment require a separately agreed scope.

Do you guarantee that AI will save a certain amount of time?

No specific result or time saving is guaranteed. Outcomes depend on the task, tool, information provided, review, and how the workflow is used.
