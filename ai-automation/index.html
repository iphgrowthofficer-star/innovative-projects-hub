AI workflow consulting

Make a repetitive process easier to manage.

Map the work before choosing a tool. Consulting helps identify suitable AI opportunities and the checks people should keep in the process.

Discuss a workflow

Start with the current process

We talk through a recurring process, the people and tools involved, what information it uses, and what a good outcome looks like. From there, we identify where a language model might assist and where human review remains important.

Possible areas to explore

  • Drafting routine email replies or follow-up messages
  • Preparing first drafts of content from approved information
  • Organizing non-sensitive notes into a checklist or summary
  • Standardizing internal instructions and repeatable procedures
  • Planning how leads or customer requests are reviewed and routed

Consulting is not the same as deployment

A workflow discussion or plan does not automatically include building, connecting, or deploying software. Tool access, permissions, testing, costs, and operational ownership must be agreed before any implementation. No automatic system is promised by a consulting conversation alone.

Start small: choose one low-risk task, test with example data, review the output, and decide whether the result is useful before expanding.

Pricing and scope for workflow consulting are discussed on a call; no new package price is being published here.
